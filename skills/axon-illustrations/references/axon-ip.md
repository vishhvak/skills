# Axon — the mascot IP

## The one-sentence description (use verbatim in every prompt)

> Axon: a small solid-black round-bodied creature with white dot eyes and no
> mouth, two branching twig-like dendrite antlers on its head, thin stick legs,
> and one long tapering tail that ends in a three-pronged synapse tuft.

The sentence is the character. Never redesign it per image; consistency across a
whole article's illustrations is what makes it an IP instead of clip art.

## Anatomy rules

- **Body**: one solid-black filled circle-ish blob, slightly taller than wide. No neck, no arms unless the action truly needs them (stubby line-arms allowed when carrying or pointing).
- **Eyes**: two white dots. Emotion comes from dot placement only (close = focused, high = alarmed). No pupils, no eyebrows, no mouth ever.
- **Dendrites**: exactly two antler-like branches on the head, each forking once or twice. They can hold, hook, or signal-catch small objects.
- **Legs**: two thin black stick legs, comically inadequate for the work being done.
- **Tail**: the signature limb. Long, tapering, ends in three short prongs. It is prehensile and conductive.

## What the tail does (the storytelling device)

The tail is why Axon exists. In every illustration it should be doing one of:

- **plugged in**: prongs socketed into a machine, jar, pipe, panel, or another element of the diagram
- **transmitting**: 2–4 small orange pulse-dots travelling along the tail, direction visible
- **bridging**: tail spanning a gap that the metaphor says must be crossed
- **probing**: tail hovering over options, prongs spread, choosing

Pulses are always **signal orange**; the tail itself stays black line/fill.

## Personality

Deadpan and earnest. Axon takes absurd work completely seriously: it will
solemnly plug its tail into a teapot labeled "ideas" and wait. It never smiles,
never panics, never poses for the viewer. It is mid-task, always.

## Hard bans

- Axon standing beside the diagram as decoration — it must perform or transmit the core action
- More than one Axon per image, unless the metaphor is literally about parallel workers
- Cute-ification: blush marks, sparkles, big anime eyes, smiles
- Speech bubbles from Axon (it has no mouth; labels are annotations, not speech)
- Redesigns: wings, hats, clothes, colors on the body
